
[[Aladoro]],

I hope you’ve managed to catch your breath after the trial I put you and the others through last week. You proved yourselves more than capable—exactly why I’m writing now.

Word has come from [[Sharn]]: specifically from [[C.A.R.E. The Coalition of Arcane Recovery and Examination]], is asking for help. There’s been a string of strange murders in the city, and the local investigators think there’s arcane involvement they can’t quite pin down. They reached out to [[Arcanix]] for students with a sharp mind and steady hearts.

Naturally, your name came to mind.

I’m sending you—to represent the Towers. 

You’re to head to the [[Panaceum]], the Jorasco Enclave in the Dragon Towers, in the upper central plateau. The healers there are working with C.A.R.E. and will fill you in on everything once you arrive.

This won’t be simple work, and it won’t be pretty. But you’ve already shown me you’re ready for something beyond classroom exercises.

Make Arcanix proud—and stay sharp.

[[Professor Tzandro Kavalant]]
